Guide chapters

Practical steps for players.

01

Find the real offer

Tap Play Now, register or log in, then open Promotions on Up X.

Ignore ads that invent amounts or percentages.

02

Read wagering first

Check the multiplier, max bet while clearing, and expiry.

If the math does not fit your bankroll, skip the promo.

03

Eligible games

Slots, crash and sports can weight differently.

Aviator may contribute less than featured slots - confirm in the terms.

04

Opt-in control

Some offers need a manual opt-in before the first deposit.

Do not assume an auto attach.

05

Crash discipline

Bonus funds plus crash chase is a fast way to burn the offer.

Set cash-out targets even when playing bonus money.

06

Sports weighting

1X2 on major leagues may count at a reduced rate.

Read the sports contribution table in the lobby.

07

Expiry clocks

Welcome paths often expire in days, not weeks.

Set a reminder so leftover wagering does not vanish.

08

KYC before cashout

Clear documents before the wagering finishes.

Name mismatches delay the first withdrawal.

09

Cash-only option

You can ignore the bonus and play with deposits only.

That is valid if you want simpler crash sessions.
